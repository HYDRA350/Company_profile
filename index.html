Who We Are?

A Global Cocoa Powder and Coffee Manufacturer based in Indonesia
Persatu.one Group (PT. Persatuone Komoditas Indonesia) is a family company and manufacturer, aiming to unite the diversity of Indonesian commodities into high-value products & brands. Founded in 2000, we started our cocoa and coffee factory in West Java to spread this blessing to the world!

Persatu.one Cocoa Factory

Located in West Java, Indonesia, we focus on COCOA POWDER production. With a production capacity up to 800 metric tons/month, we supply Alkalized Cocoa Powder, Natural Cocoa Powder, and Hot Chocolate Mix for your business.

Persatu.one Coffee Factory

Located in Banten, Indonesia, we produce Arabica and Robusta roasted coffee beans from more than 20 origins worldwide. We also produce instant coffee, supply green beans, and help coffee shops around the world get the best coffee from Indonesia!
